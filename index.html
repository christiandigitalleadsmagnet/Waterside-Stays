How it works

One visit, one clear report.

  1. Tell us the dates

    Send the check-out time, the next check-in and how we get access. One-off visits or a regular slot after every stay.

  2. We inspect and photograph

    Every room, in the same order every time, against your own inventory. Anything broken, missing or dirty is photographed close up.

  3. You get the report

    A dated PDF with photos and condition notes, sent the same day. Problems that need you are flagged at the top.

Questions

Before you book

How quickly do I get the report?

The same day as the visit, usually within a few hours. Anything urgent, like a leak or damage that stops the next booking, we phone or message you about straight away.

How do you get into the property?

By key safe, smart lock or a key we hold. You tell us the method when you book, and we keep access details private.

Can the report be used for an Airbnb damage claim?

Yes. Photos are dated and timestamped, and the check-in and check-out reports sit side by side, which is the evidence Airbnb's resolution process asks for.

Do you clean or fix things?

We inspect and report. On a guest callout we sort small problems on the spot, like resetting a boiler or the router, and arrange a tradesperson for anything bigger with your approval.

Is there a contract?

One-off visits need no contract. Regular clients sign a short service agreement covering visits, access, photos and payment.
